# Portfolio Summary

## Project Title
Critical Thinking Applied to Customer Churn

## One-line description
End-to-end Data Science project where I reframed a high-pressure churn request, rigorously evaluated the data, and delivered a transparent risk score plus an interpretable Logistic Regression baseline instead of rushing to a complex model.

## What I demonstrated
- Problem reframing under stakeholder pressure and time constraints
- Structured data evaluation (base rates, missingness, segment analysis)
- Multi-hypothesis testing and a disconfirming-evidence mindset
- Decision to prioritize interpretability (rule-based score + Logistic Regression)
- Clear communication of uncertainty and recommended next actions
- End-to-end thinking from diagnosis to a deployable Streamlit app

## Technical elements
- Synthetic data generation with known ground-truth drivers
- Pandas-based exploratory and segment analysis
- Transparent rule-based risk score
- Logistic Regression (interpretable baseline) with coefficient analysis
- Streamlit app for interactive presentation
