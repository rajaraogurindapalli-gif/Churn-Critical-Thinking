# 01 – Problem Definition

## Business Problem (Stated)
Leadership observed monthly churn rising from ~4% to ~7%.  
They want a churn prediction model within 10 days and believe the recent price increase is the main cause.

## Critical Thinking Reframe
**Stated problem:** “Build a model that predicts who will churn because of the price increase.”  
**Better problem:** “Why did churn increase, which drivers are strongest and actionable, and what should we do next under time pressure?”

### Why reframe?
- The stated version locks us into one solution (a model) and one hypothesis (price).
- Critical thinking requires us to question the framing before investing effort.

## Success Criteria
- Clear diagnosis of the main drivers (with evidence and uncertainty).
- Actionable recommendations.
- A simple, transparent risk score or interpretable model.
- Documented limitations and next experiments.

## Constraints
- Tight deadline.
- Incomplete or imperfect data.
- Multiple stakeholders.
- Need to avoid the urgency trap.

## Key Questions
1. What is the exact definition of churn?
2. What else changed around the same time as the price increase?
3. Which segments show the largest lift?
4. Is the price effect primary or secondary?
5. What is the minimum viable deliverable that improves decisions?
