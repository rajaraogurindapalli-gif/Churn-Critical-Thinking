# 05 – Critical Thinking Analysis

## Process followed
1. Reflective urgency – allocate time for diagnosis despite the deadline.
2. Reframe the stated problem.
3. Generate competing hypotheses (price, usage, UI change, support, new customers).
4. Test with simple quantitative evidence (base rates and segments).
5. Look for disconfirming evidence.
6. Choose the minimum viable, explainable deliverable.

## Outcome
Price was a real but secondary factor. Low usage, especially combined with the UI change, was stronger. The final recommendation prioritized a transparent score and targeted investigation over a rushed complex model.
